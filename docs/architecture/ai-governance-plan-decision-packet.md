# AI Governance Plan — architecture decision packet (AVS-GOV-1)

**Status:** future implementation design; not implemented by this documentation change. **Product boundary:** AI Value Studio (AVS) is standalone. The AI Governance Plan is a **generated advisory deliverable, not a governance platform**. It is a client document alongside Executive Brief and Pilot Charter, produced from the existing use-case wizard. It does not approve use cases, enforce controls, track control evidence, run an audit-trail product, provide an operator workspace, or depend on another platform.

## 1. Decision record and existing boundary

| Decision | Rationale and implementation impact |
|---|---|
| Generate a French-first, descriptive/advisory AI Governance Plan in **Markdown and PDF only**. | `backend/app/documents/service.py` already routes Jinja Markdown/HTML through WeasyPrint for PDF. DOCX is out of scope; keep the existing storage, versioning, format and API contracts. |
| Build a deterministic section model from a locked, submitted `AssessmentVersion`; attach a matching `ScorecardVersion` if one exists. No review or document approval dependency for **either** format. | The existing service requires a matching scorecard and submitted review, and gates PDF on an approved review; those conditions must remain for Executive Brief and Pilot Charter but be bypassed for this type. Missing advisory answers become open decisions, not new submission/generation blockers. Never use a mutable draft. |
| Treat legal and framework references as conditional review guidance, not legal determinations. | The EU AI Act category is indicative, based solely on captured inputs and curated rules. No assertion of applicability, compliance, legal status, or certification. Render the exact notice on the first page and in Markdown: **« Document d’aide à la décision — à valider avec le service juridique, le DPO et les responsables compétents. Il ne constitue pas un avis juridique. »** |
| Store provenance on each immutable document version, not as product audit functionality. | Record template and rules-catalogue versions, source assessment/scorecard version IDs and numbers (scorecard nullable), input question references, generation timestamp, and paragraph/section IDs assisted by an LLM. Keep the generated artifact and SHA-256 tied to that version; no evidence ledger or new audit tables. |
| Optional LLM prose only after deterministic construction. | The complete document works without a provider. The LLM cannot classify risk, select obligations, set risk values, invent owners, or change source references. |

**Alternatives rejected:** a governance dashboard/workflow engine and approval gates (wrong product); runtime rule enforcement and evidence tracking (not a document); external legal lookup at generation time (unversioned, unstable claims); DOCX/browser-side PDF (no current pipeline); LLM-only classification (unverifiable). **Trade-offs:** curated rules need periodic expert review; conservative unknowns produce less decisive prose; no scorecard means score-dependent facts stay unreported/unknown, but generation remains available. Explicit metadata and catalogue validation add maintenance work in the future coding slices.

The current baseline is `backend/app/wizard/templates/baseline_fr.yaml` (`baseline-fr`, version 1; JSON-formatted template), `backend/app/documents/service.py`, `backend/app/documents/templates/fr/`, `backend/app/db/models/lifecycle.py`, `backend/app/schemas/document.py`, `backend/app/api/v1/documents.py`, and `backend/app/llm/contracts.py`. `SUPPORTED_DOCUMENT_TYPES` currently includes only `ExecutiveBrief` and `PilotCharter`; `AMOAImplementationPlan` exists as an enum value but is not supported by that service. This packet is the authoritative **future deliverable** design, not a claim that the third document exists today. The older MVP packet at `docs/architecture/decision-packet.md` describes different historical product scope; for this deliverable, the decisions here take precedence.

## 2. French client document blueprint

**Common rendering contract:** every section has a stable section ID, facts marked **Faits déclarés** with wizard/scorecard references, and separate **Hypothèses / recommandations** with catalogue-rule IDs where applicable. Display `Non renseigné / à confirmer` beside a missing input; do not render a missing answer as “non”, “aucun”, zero, or a named owner. Conditional guidance reads “À examiner si …”, never “obligation applicable” without confirmed scope. The notice above appears independently of any LLM. An optional LLM may polish only the indicated prose, not headings, notices, tables, rule results, quoted answers, or source IDs.

|---|---|---|

| 2. **Classification du risque IA du cas d’usage au regard de l’EU AI Act** | Scope (`jurisdictions_in_scope`, `eu_market_or_eu_deployer`), `intended_domain`, `affected_person_decision`, `autonomy_level` and versioned risk-tier indicators; show one **indicative** category and triggered rule IDs/answer keys: *pratiques interdites / haut risque / transparence ou risque limité / risque minimal*. | 3. **Cartographie des référentiels et obligations** | Conditional catalogue rows for NIST AI RMF **Govern / Map / Measure / Manage**, ISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act and Morocco **loi 09-08 / CNDP**, including cross-border transfer context; use jurisdiction, personal-data and transfer answers. ## 4. Deterministic rules, catalogues, templates and LLM

**Future paths:** put the French templates at `backend/app/documents/templates/fr/ai_governance_plan.md.j2` and `backend/app/documents/templates/fr/ai_governance_plan.html.j2`, reusing `backend/app/documents/templates/fr/pdf.css` and `DocumentService`'s Jinja/WeasyPrint path. Pin each released template pair by content hash and template version; retain immutable old pairs under `backend/app/documents/templates/fr/releases/<template-version>/` when revising the active pair so an old version remains reproducible. Put structured, immutable, version-named catalogue data under `backend/app/governance_plan/catalogues/` and deterministic evaluation/section assembly in `backend/app/governance_plan/`. These are proposed paths, **not files added by this PR**. Keep FR text in the template/catalogue; don't hardcode legal or RACI wording in Python or in prompts.

One coherent catalogue release has a stable `catalogue_version` and `schema_version`; each entry has a unique stable `id`, entry `version`, French `text_fr`, `section_id`, `kind` (risk indicator, candidate obligation, framework guidance, RACI default, risk seed, test expectation, KPI or roadmap action), declared source/reference and review date, `applies_if` over allowed typed wizard keys, and `on_unknown` review wording. Risk entries additionally have tier/priority and required positive indicators; RACI entries have role/activity and suggested R/A/C/I; risk/KPI/test/roadmap entries carry suggested measure or action, not completion status. Validate IDs, schema, referenced question keys, allowed operators/enum values, release compatibility and French copy at build/test time. Ship curated NIST AI RMF Govern/Map/Measure/Manage, ISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act, and Morocco loi 09-08/CNDP/transfer guidance as reviewed data. No external legal reference retrieval at generation time; updating catalogue/review date is a separate controlled content change.

**Applicability semantics:** evaluate predicates in three states (`true`, `false`, `unknown`). Emit a statement of a catalogue entry only on `true`; on `false` omit it, on `unknown` emit only its labelled review prompt and missing keys. An explicit `unknown`, missing field, unsupported enum/country, conflicting answers or `Other` never evaluates to `false` or a legal conclusion. Existing `bad_answer_impact` and scorecard `risk_profile` measure business exposure, **not** EU AI Act classification. Free-text `ai_task`, `compliance_constraints` or `prohibited_behaviour` may be quoted as declared context, never parsed as authoritative legal indicators.

**Classification precedence:** for confirmed EU context and sufficient structured intended-use evidence, evaluate positive *prohibited-practice* indicators first, then *high-risk* intended-use indicators, then *transparency/limited-risk* indicators, else *minimal-risk* only if all needed indicators were actually resolved false. A matching higher tier wins. For example, confirmed EU scope + `intended_domain=recruitment_screening` (and affected-person screening intent) yields a **candidate** high-risk review prompt; confirmed EU customer-facing generative-support intent yields a transparency review prompt; sensitive health data alone is not proof of a statutory high-risk category. No existing baseline question explicitly captures a prohibited practice as an intended behavior (`prohibited_behaviour` describes what should **not** happen): the prohibited branch must not fire from keywords, severity or a blank field. It remains dormant until an unambiguous structured positive indicator is supplied under a reviewed catalogue/schema; ambiguous cases are *indéterminé / à confirmer*, not minimal-risk. If EU scope is not established, describe the EU taxonomy as not yet assessable and seek scope confirmation rather than silently issuing an EU classification. Every tier is indicative, with matched rule IDs/input keys and “à confirmer par le juridique/DPO”.

Construct a typed deterministic section model with stable IDs, rendered facts, assumptions, recommendations, sources, rule outcomes and missing keys. The Markdown/HTML templates consume that **same** model; even with no provider, it yields all 12 sections, useful tables/bullets and explicit unknown markers. Fail closed on a missing or incompatible template/catalogue release (visible generation error; no stored partial artifact or misleading version).

**Optional narrative adapter:** extend and use the existing `LlmProvider` contract in `backend/app/llm/contracts.py` with a future typed governance-plan narrative operation and request/response schema; do not call a provider directly from templates. Pass only minimized, redacted section facts/recommendations and stable references, never the whole assessment, personal records, secrets or raw evidence. Provider returns structured paragraph IDs, French prose and unmodified source references. Validate response size, structure, IDs, references and semantic constraints against the deterministic model; reject attempts to replace a classification, applicability, risk rating, obligation, owner, number, notice or source. If unavailable, timed out or invalid, log/report outcome metadata and render the deterministic section instead; generation succeeds without pretending the LLM ran. Mark accepted assisted paragraphs in the artifact and `ai_assisted_sections_json`, including section/paragraph IDs and source references; also retain markers for AI-assisted *input answers* as distinct provenance. No LLM call is required for a complete document.

## 6. Security, operations and failure contract

- Minimize/redact LLM narrative payloads: suppress names, sensitive categories' sample values, personal identifiers, case text, secrets and raw evidence when not needed to rephrase; allow only bounded summaries/IDs. Prompt content is untrusted data, never provider instructions. Claim zero retention/no training **only if** the selected provider contract actually guarantees it; otherwise flag provider retention, data residency and transfer terms as an explicit procurement/DPO open decision, not an AVS compliance promise.
- Escape all answer and catalogue values in HTML/PDF (existing Jinja HTML autoescape), and escape or neutralize untrusted Markdown/HTML before it can render as active markup. Do not accept raw HTML from answers, catalogue entries or the LLM. Validate templates, rule predicates, reference IDs and provenance shapes before release.
- Unknown rule, unknown country, conflicting scope or unknown policy yields a French review prompt, not a legal classification. A missing/incompatible catalogue or template is a distinct operator-visible generation error; never substitute a silently different release or save a partial/incorrect claim. Provider configuration, timeout and invalid output instead yield the **complete deterministic fallback**, with explicit fallback reason in metadata/logs and no false LLM marker.
- Conditional phrasing for residency and transfers: `cross_border_transfer_expected=yes` means “transfert envisagé — analyse juridique/DPO à conduire”, not proof of authorization; `no` is only a declared expectation, not verified absence. Do not promise compliance with EU or Moroccan law.
